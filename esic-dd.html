Eligibility

  • Degree of a recognised university
  • 3 years' experience in administration, accounts, marketing, public relations, insurance, revenue or tax matters in a government department, PSU or autonomous body
  • Desirable: Subordinate Accounts Service, ICWA, MBA (Finance) or a law degree; experience in social insurance or labour laws
  • Upper age 35 years

Age relaxation (2021 notice): SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3, ex-servicemen 5, Central Government servants and ESIC employees up to 5, PwBD 10 (up to 56 years).

FAQ

ESIC Deputy Director — common questions

Is the ESIC Deputy Director 2026 notification out?

No. As of October 2026 UPSC has not notified a new ESIC Deputy Director recruitment. The latest was Advertisement No. 55/2021 for 151 posts, with the Recruitment Test held on 17 July 2022.

What is the ESIC Deputy Director exam pattern?

A 2-hour objective Recruitment Test for 300 marks with one-third negative marking: Part A General English and Part B General Ability (Hindi and English). It was reported as 120 questions. Shortlisted candidates face a 100-mark interview; RT and interview are weighted 75:25.

What is the ESIC Deputy Director syllabus?

Part A: General English. Part B: Principles of HRM, Marketing Management, Accounting & Financial Management, RTI Act, Numerical Ability & Logical Reasoning, Computer Applications, General Science, Public Administration and Development Issues, Freedom Movement & Indian Union, and Current Events.

What was the ESIC Deputy Director cut-off?

In the 2022 Recruitment Test the cut-offs out of 300 were UR 200.87, OBC 192.23, EWS 168.10, SC 182.77 and ST 169.84. 13,555 candidates appeared.

Who is eligible for ESIC Deputy Director?

Graduates with 3 years' experience in administration, accounts, marketing, public relations, insurance, revenue or tax matters in government, a PSU or an autonomous body, up to 35 years of age (with category relaxations).

Is the ESIC Deputy Director syllabus the same as DRDO SAO?

Yes. The 2021 ESIC Deputy Director notice lists the same 10 Part B topics plus General English that UPSC used for the DRDO Senior Administrative Officer tests in 2022 and 2024.
