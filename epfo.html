Eligibility

  • APFC: degree of a recognised university; a diploma in Company Law, Labour Laws or Public Administration is desirable
  • APFC age: up to 35 (UR/EWS), 38 (OBC), 40 (SC/ST)
  • EO/AO: bachelor's degree in any subject
  • EO/AO age (2025): up to 30 (UR/EWS), 33 (OBC), 35 (SC/ST), 40 (PwBD)

Relaxations: PwBD 10 years (up to 56), EPFO employees and Central Government servants up to 5 years, ex-servicemen 5 years. Fee ₹25 per post; no fee for SC, ST, PwBD and women.

Exam pattern

EPFO APFC & EO/AO exam pattern

Both posts use the same Recruitment Test format, followed by an interview.

Maximum marks300
Duration2 hours
QuestionsObjective MCQs, all equal marks — 120 questions as reported
Negative marking⅓ of the marks for each wrong answer; no penalty for unanswered
LanguageEnglish and Hindi
Interview100 marks
WeightageRT : Interview = 75 : 25
Min. interview marks50 (UR/EWS), 45 (OBC), 40 (SC/ST/PwBD)

EPFO APFC syllabus 2026

The nine topics listed in UPSC Advt 52/2026. The 2025 advertisement used the same list for both APFC and EO/AO.

General Science and Basic Knowledge of Computer Applications

7

Industrial Relations, Labour Codes and Social Security in India

The 2025 syllabus said "Labour Laws"; 2026 says "Labour Codes".

8

Principles of Accountancy, Auditing and Insurance

9

Current Events of National and International Importance

Labour Codes: the 2026 syllabus names the Labour Codes instead of the old labour laws, so prepare the four codes (Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, and Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions).

FAQ

EPFO APFC & EO/AO — common questions

When is the EPFO APFC 2026 exam?

UPSC will hold the Recruitment Test for Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner (Advt 52/2026) on 20 December 2026.

How many APFC vacancies are there in 2026?

80 posts: UR 29, EWS 8, OBC 18, SC 15 and ST 10, including 3 for PwBD candidates. Applications closed on 11 September 2026.

Is there an EPFO EO/AO recruitment in 2026?

Not so far. The 2026 UPSC advertisement covers APFC only. The last EO/AO recruitment was Advt 52/2025 with 156 posts and a combined test on 30 November 2025.

What is the EPFO APFC exam pattern?

A 2-hour objective Recruitment Test for 300 marks in English and Hindi, with one-third negative marking. Shortlisted candidates face a 100-mark interview; the test and interview are weighted 75:25.

What is the EPFO APFC syllabus?

General English; Indian Culture, Heritage and Freedom Movements; Developmental Issues and Indian Economy; Governance and Constitution; General Science and Computers; Elementary Mathematics, Statistics and Mental Ability; Industrial Relations, Labour Codes and Social Security; Accountancy, Auditing and Insurance; and Current Events.

What was the EPFO APFC cut-off?

In the 2023 Recruitment Test the APFC cut-offs out of 300 were UR 178.33, EWS 176.68, OBC 178.33, SC 156.68 and ST 151.68. For EO/AO, UR was 138.33.

What is the age limit for EPFO APFC?

35 years for UR/EWS, 38 for OBC and 40 for SC/ST, with further relaxation for PwBD, EPFO employees, Central Government servants and ex-servicemen.
