Vacancy & eligibility

DRDO SAO recruitment 2026 (Advt 04/2026)

Senior Administrative Officer Grade-II is a Group 'A' gazetted post in DRDO, Ministry of Defence, filled by UPSC through a Recruitment Test (RT) and interview.

Eligibility

  • Bachelor's degree from a recognised university
  • 3 years' experience in administration, establishment or accounts work
  • Upper age 35 (UR/EWS), 38 (OBC), 40 (SC/ST); PwBD up to 10 years more

Syllabus

DRDO SAO syllabus (2022 & 2024 tests)

UPSC used the same topic list in both recent tests. It is a general-ability paper with a management layer — not a rules/CCS paper.

PART A
A

General English

Understanding of the language and workmanlike use of words — vocabulary, grammar, usage, comprehension.

PART B — GENERAL ABILITY
1

Principles of Human Resource Management

HR planning, recruitment, training, performance, compensation, industrial relations, OB.

2

Principles of Marketing Management

Marketing concept & mix, consumer behaviour, STP, product, pricing, distribution, promotion.

3

Principles of Accounting & Financial Management

Accounting basics, final accounts, ratios, costing, budgeting, capital budgeting, working capital.

4

Right to Information Act

Provisions, procedure, exemptions, Information Commissions, penalties, amendments.

5

Numerical Ability & Logical Reasoning

Arithmetic, data interpretation and verbal/non-verbal reasoning.

6

Fundamentals of Computer Application

Hardware/software, MS Office, internet, networking, cyber security.

7

General Science

Everyday physics, chemistry and biology; science & technology.

8

Public Administration & Development Issues

Principles of administration, Indian administration, governance, schemes, economy.

9

Freedom Movement & Indian Union

Freedom struggle, Constitution, Union government and polity.

10

Current Events of National & International Importance

Last 12–18 months: national, international, economy, defence & DRDO.

Advt 04/2026 does not print a separate syllabus. We will update this page the moment UPSC issues the RT notice.

FAQ

DRDO SAO — common questions

When is the DRDO SAO Grade-II recruitment test?

UPSC hasn't announced it yet. In the last cycle the advertisement came in July 2023 and the test was held on 9 March 2024, so a test in early 2027 is likely for Advt 04/2026. We'll update this page as soon as the RT notice is out.

What is the DRDO SAO exam pattern?

The last recruitment test had 120 objective questions for 300 marks (2.5 marks each), 2 hours, with one-third negative marking. Part A is General English (English only); Part B is General Ability (English and Hindi).

What was the DRDO SAO 2024 cut-off?

For the 20-post cycle (test on 9 March 2024) the reported cut-offs out of 300 were UR 167.50, OBC 160.83, EWS 130.83, SC 147.50 and ST 130.00.

Is the DRDO SAO exam about CCS rules, GFR and FR-SR?

No. The 2022 and 2024 tests followed the general-ability syllabus — English, HRM, marketing, accounting, RTI, reasoning, computers, science, public administration, freedom movement and current events. Service rules matter more at the interview stage.
