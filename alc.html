UPSC Assistant Labour Commissioner (ALC) exam guide

Eligibility

  • Degree of a recognised university
  • Diploma in Social Work, Labour Welfare, Industrial Relations, Personnel Management or Labour Law
  • Desirable: degree in Law
  • Upper age 35 years

Usual UPSC relaxations apply: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3, PwBD 10, ex-servicemen 5, Central Government servants up to 5. Fee ₹25; no fee for SC, ST, PwBD and women.

Syllabus

ALC syllabus

The topics in UPSC's notice for the 20 August 2023 Recruitment Test. Unlike the DRDO SAO or ESIC tests, this paper is mostly labour law.

1

Constitutional provisions relating to labour

Labour in the Concurrent List; Fundamental Rights for workers, employers and industry; Directive Principles on worker welfare, social security, women and children.

2

Industrial relations

Trade Unions Act, 1926; Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946; Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

3

Social security

Employees' Compensation Act, 1923; ESI Act, 1948; Unorganised Workers' Social Security Act, 2008; Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972; Maternity Benefit Act, 1961.

4

Labour welfare

Factories Act, 1948; Contract Labour (Regulation & Abolition) Act, 1970; Inter-State Migrant Workmen Act, 1979.

5

Building and other construction workers

BOCW (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996; BOCW Welfare Cess Act, 1996.

6

Wages and bonus

Payment of Wages Act, 1936; Minimum Wages Act, 1948; Payment of Bonus Act, 1965.

7

The four Labour Codes

Code on Wages, 2019; Industrial Relations Code, 2020; Code on Social Security, 2020; Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020.

8

Trade union movement

9

General mental ability

10

Current events of national and international importance

Prepare the Labour Codes well: UPSC's 2026 EPFO syllabus already says "Labour Codes" instead of "Labour Laws", so expect the four Codes to carry more weight in the next ALC test. Industrial relations and social security also overlap with the EPFO APFC syllabus.

FAQ

Assistant Labour Commissioner — common questions

Is the UPSC Assistant Labour Commissioner 2026 notification out?

No. As of October 2026 UPSC has not advertised a new ALC recruitment. The latest was Advertisement No. 21/2022 for 29 Central Labour Service posts, with the Recruitment Test on 20 August 2023 and the final result on 21 March 2024.

What is the ALC exam pattern?

A 2-hour objective Recruitment Test for 300 marks in English and Hindi, with one-third negative marking (reported as 120 questions). Shortlisted candidates face a 100-mark interview, and the test and interview are weighted 75:25.

What is the UPSC ALC syllabus?

Constitutional provisions on labour, industrial relations laws, social security laws, labour welfare laws, building and construction workers laws, wages and bonus laws, the four Labour Codes, the trade union movement, general mental ability and current events.

What was the ALC cut-off?

UPSC hasn't published the 2023 cut-off in the notices we checked. In the 2017 Recruitment Test the reported cut-offs out of 300 were General 191.50, OBC 174.50, SC 166.00 and ST 144.50.

Who is eligible for the Assistant Labour Commissioner post?

Graduates who also hold a diploma in Social Work, Labour Welfare, Industrial Relations, Personnel Management or Labour Law, up to 35 years of age with category relaxations. A law degree is desirable.

Which posts are filled through the ALC recruitment?

Junior Time Scale posts of the Central Labour Service: Assistant Labour Commissioner (Central), Assistant Welfare Commissioner, Assistant Labour Welfare Commissioner and Assistant Director.
