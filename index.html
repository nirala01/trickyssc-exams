FAQ

DRDO SAO course — common questions

Is there an online course for the DRDO SAO 2027 exam?

Yes. The TrickySSC Exams DRDO SAO Grade-II 2027 course is built only for the UPSC recruitment test for Senior Administrative Officer Grade-II in DRDO. It has 11 study PDFs, 140 subject-wise tests of 50 questions and 10 full-length mock tests. It opens in December 2026 and you can pre-register now.

What is the fee for the DRDO SAO course?

₹999 if you pre-register before 31 December 2026, and ₹1499 after that. Pre-registering is free; you pay once online by UPI, card or net banking when the course opens.

Are there free DRDO SAO mock tests?

Yes. Test 1 of every subject (14 tests of 50 questions) and Full Mock 1 (120 questions) are free, along with a free sample chapter from the study PDFs.

Which subjects does the DRDO SAO course cover?

Human Resource Management, Marketing Management, Financial Management, Accounting, RTI Act, Polity, Public Administration & Development Issues, Indian Freedom Movement, General Science, Computer and Current Affairs (study PDFs plus tests), and General English, Numerical Ability and Logical Reasoning (tests).

Are the DRDO SAO mock tests in the real exam pattern?

Yes. Each full mock has 120 questions for 300 marks in 2 hours with one-third negative marking, split into Part A (General English) and Part B (General Ability), the pattern UPSC used in the 2024 recruitment test.

How many DRDO SAO vacancies are there in 2026?

UPSC Advertisement No. 04/2026 has 29 posts of Senior Administrative Officer Grade-II in DRDO at Pay Level 10. Applications closed on 29 May 2026 and the recruitment test date is yet to be announced.
